Landslides

The Landslides project addresses the recording of measurements related to landslides. The project is made for the Dionýz Štúr State Geological Institute.

The project is a web application programmed with Go, VueJS, PostgreSQL, PostGIS technologies and manages a database of measurements related to landslides.In total, there are 20 different types of measurements in more than 100 regions of Slovakia, such as geodetic measurements, insar measurements, water levels and flows, micromorphological displacements, meteorological data, etc. Hundreds of thousands of measurements are involved of which some are recorded automatically. All these measurements are evaluated and can be viewed in dozens of graphs and maps that are part of the application. Part of the app is also available to the public from www.geology.sk
