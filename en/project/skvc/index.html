Slovakia in Numbers

Slovakia in Numbers (Slovensko v číslach) gathers data scattered across the internet and public administration and brings it together in one place, in a clear form – as figures, charts and maps.

Website: slovenskovcislach.sk

Project description

At the heart of the project is the analysis of open public-administration datasets on Slovakia.

Figures and visualisations give an overview of society, the economy, the environment and other areas, and help address public-administration challenges.

Audience and data sources

Who it is for:

  • internet users
  • businesses
  • the scientific community
  • students
  • foreigners

Data sources:

  • public authorities
  • government agencies
  • non-profit organisations

How the portal is designed

  • Data collection: intended to be as automated as possible, with semi-automated and manual data entry also foreseen.
  • Database and archive: data is stored in a database and archived, so trends over time can be tracked even for data that is not yet archived anywhere else.
  • Processing: a combination of text and numerical processing.
  • Visualisation: charts and maps with a special focus on the breakdown by territorial unit – down to municipalities or other trackable units where the data allows.
  • Further use: the data and outputs (graphical and tabular) can be reused in many other applications and publications.
