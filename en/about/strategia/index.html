About

Company strategy

How AXON PRO, s.r.o. approaches quality, IT service management, information security and the environment – our core strategic aims.

Who we are

  • Company type: a limited liability company specialising in high-value IT services.
  • Core business: custom software development – from analysis through the solution and its implementation to contracted support – and comprehensive solutions for medium-sized and large companies and organisations.
  • Market position: one of the key players on the Slovak IT market and a supplier of software solutions to several government institutions, either directly or as a subcontractor to the main contractor of a larger project.
  • Priority: customer support. Our focus on customer needs drives the continuous improvement of our processes.

Quality policy

Management regards the quality policy as one of the main elements of the company’s sound development.

We aim to offer solutions and services that satisfy customers in terms of technology, quality and price. Continuously improving the quality of our processes is one of the building blocks of sound management and of delivering solutions and services with high added value.

Principles our employees follow

  • Objective: deliver services on agreed deadlines, at the agreed quality and at reasonable cost – to the satisfaction of customers, employees and owners alike.
  • Approach: efficient, productive and stable operations in compliance with applicable law, and continuous improvement of the sub-processes that affect product quality.
  • Tool: an effective quality management system (QMS) and IT service management system (ITSM), integrated into a single management system across all levels of the company.
  • Purpose: the integrated system helps us meet our objectives, ensure service quality and uphold information security in all our activities.
  • Prevention: preventing nonconformities takes precedence over detecting them.
  • Partnership: with our major suppliers and partners we build mutual trust, respect and a shared commitment to our customers.
  • Measure: we measure the quality of our work by the satisfaction of all our customers.

Service management strategy

Management is committed to extending the quality management system and building a transparent IT service management system with defined metrics for each service, one that is also attractive to customers.

What we want to achieve

  • Service portfolio: one that satisfies customers from the government and public sector as well as the most demanding private-sector clients, and supports their business processes.
  • SLAs: mutually beneficial and clearly worded, so that they also motivate customers’ management to create the conditions for high-quality cooperation.
  • Trust and standards: proof for customers that every defined service will be delivered on the basis of mutual trust and to pre-defined standards.

How we do it

  • We implement ITSM in line with ISO/IEC 20000-1 and develop it based on industry best practice.
  • We base our processes on ITIL recommendations for service and support in the delivery of solutions and services.

We place particular emphasis on the help desk and customer support processes, change management and information security – we currently regard these core ITSM processes as key to a high level of service.

Management is committed to actively supporting all IT service management processes so that the set objectives for expanding our services are met, above all in the areas that matter to our customers.

Key prerequisites

Management system

  • Build, operate and continuously improve a management system based on meeting customers’ actual and expected requirements.
  • Build an integrated management system based on the international standards ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO/IEC 20000 (ITSM).

Innovation

  • Continuously innovate products and services in line with developments in IT.
  • Keep improving our services and adopt promising new technologies with better parameters.

Security policy in ITSM

Our security policy covers both information security management within the organisation and service delivery in ITSM.

We currently serve mainly customers in the government, public and financial sectors, where information security is especially important. We therefore reduce the risks to integrity, confidentiality and availability in our services to the lowest possible level.

Rules and responsibilities

Clear rules: we follow clear instructions and rules, and assign security responsibilities explicitly so that everyone is aware of them.

The whole ITSM life cycle: the policy applies primarily to the organisation, but it emphasises the entire ITSM process life cycle and compliance with information security rules by employees working in ITSM.

Training: security training for these employees is an important part of our HR policy.

Risk management

  • Foundation: the analysis and assessment of risks for each service and setting appropriate countermeasures against security threats.
  • Methodology: we use a methodology that keeps the results of repeated risk analyses comparable.
  • Unacceptable risk: we have defined a level of unacceptable risk; all risks above it are managed through countermeasure programmes.

Obligations and commitments

  • Every employee must report any weakness, system error, threat or security incident immediately to the person appointed by management as responsible for the ISMS.
  • Consistent adherence to these principles is one of the main steps towards ensuring and maintaining the functionality, availability, confidentiality and security of the entire ISMS and ITSM.
  • Conditions for ensuring the integrity, confidentiality and availability of customer information are an integral part of our SLAs.
  • We take into account all legislative and regulatory requirements and regularly review their impact on individual information systems and processes.
  • Any intentional threat to the security of information within ITSM at AXON PRO, s.r.o., at our customers or suppliers, or to the quality of our services is treated as a breach of discipline or of the law.

Management is committed to actively supporting IT service management and information security both inside and outside the company, and to providing the necessary resources to achieve all objectives in this area.

Environmental policy

Our commitments

In the environmental field we are committed to:

  • Developing and implementing an effective environmental management system in line with the international standard ISO 14001.
  • Strictly complying with environmental legal requirements.
  • Considering the environmental impact of our activities.
  • Continuous improvement and pollution prevention.
  • Documenting the relevant measures and procedures in the AXON PRO Quality Manual and the related organisational and technical procedures (guidelines).

Binding force and monitoring

The environmental policy is binding on all AXON PRO employees.

Managers must apply it in practice and take effective measures when developing and implementing the environmental management system.

Compliance is monitored by the Environmental Officer.

Conclusion

Management will consistently enforce this strategy at company management level and lead all employees and external collaborators to follow it. It therefore calls on every employee to contribute actively to the further development of the company’s services.
